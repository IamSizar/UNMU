# App Design Brief: HalalStocks \n\n## 1. App Summary\n**App Name:** HalalStocks (Halal Stock Scanner & Investment Companion)\n\nHalalStocks is a premium, modern fintech and Islamic investing mobile application. It empowers Muslim investors by allowing them to discover global stocks and automatically screen them for Shariah compliance (analyzing company activities, debt ratios, and haram income sources). \n\nBeyond a simple screener, the app acts as a complete investment companion where users can track their portfolios, monitor market sentiment (like the Fear & Greed index), and use practical Islamic finance tools such as a Zakat calculator and a Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) calculator.\n\n## 2. Brand Positioning\nThe app's branding should convey **trust, financial security, modern technology, and Islamic values** without looking outdated. It is positioned as a top-tier financial tool, inspired by industry leaders like Revolut, N26, and Apple Wallet.\n\n## 3. Design Style & Aesthetic Guidelines\n1. **Vibe:** Minimalist, clean, modern, and slightly playful.\n2. **Visual Rules (Crucial):** \n   - We follow a strict **whitespace-first** approach.\n   - The logo should be flat or semi-flat.\n   - **NO** heavy gradients, **NO** complex 3D textures, and **NO** noisy backgrounds.\n   - The logo must look sharp, clean, and highly legible at very small sizes (e.g., as an iOS/Android home screen icon).\n3. **Concept Ideas:** The logo should elegantly merge the concepts of wealth generation/investing (e.g., growth charts, financial security, abstract upward movement) with subtle, modern Islamic motifs, keeping it sophisticated and abstract.\n\n## 4. Brand Color Palette\nThe application uses a high-contrast, premium \"Dark Mode\" aesthetic. The logo and app icon should utilize or complement these exact hex codes:\n\n* **Primary / Base:** **Midnight Blue (`#0A1628`)**\n  * *Meaning:* Trust, authority, security, and a premium financial foundation.\n* **Primary Accent:** **Cyan (`#00D9FF`)**\n  * *Meaning:* Modern technology, clarity, forward movement, and active elements.\n* **Secondary Accent:** **Gold (`#FFD700`)**\n  * *Meaning:* Used sparingly for premium features, wealth, and high-quality status.\n\n> **Note for the Designer:** A solid Midnight Blue background with a Cyan and/or Gold minimalist icon on top would perfectly match the current in-app UI.\n
